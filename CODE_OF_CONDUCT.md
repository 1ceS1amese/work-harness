# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Work Harness contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, professional, and
harassment-free community. Participation should be safe regardless of background, identity,
experience, ability, appearance, nationality, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Accept technical disagreement without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent, and project security boundaries.
- Help newcomers understand project conventions and review feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Insults, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Using project spaces to promote abuse, malware, credential theft, or unsafe deployment.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, community events, and when
someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed
to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory channel.
Include only the information needed to investigate. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy
as far as practical and will not tolerate retaliation.
